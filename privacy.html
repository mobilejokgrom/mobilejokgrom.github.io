Privacy Policy

App Name: Lengu (English Cards Jokgrom)

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

Lengu does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your data is stored locally on your device or in your personal Google account.

2. Google API Usage

The app uses Google Sign-In and Google APIs (Drive API, Sheets API) for the following purposes:

  • Google Drive: To save and load your vocabulary lists
  • Google Sheets: To export and import your vocabulary data

We only request access to files created by the app itself.

3. Data Security

All data synced with Google services is stored in your personal Google account. We do not have access to your data and do not transmit it to any third-party servers.

4. Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

  • Internet access: For Google Sign-In and syncing with Google Drive/Sheets
  • Google Account: For authentication and data synchronization

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can revoke app access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings
  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the application
